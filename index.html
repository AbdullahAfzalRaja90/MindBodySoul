Background & Education

Dr. Iqbal combines scientific understanding with compassionate care to address the root causes of issues rather than just treating symptoms. Hypnosis: A Source of Transformation

What is Hypnotherapy?

Hypnosis is a powerful tool for accessing your subconscious mind to create lasting change. It's a focused, relaxed state where your subconscious becomes open to positive suggestions for personal transformation and growth.

What to Expect in a Session

You'll experience deep relaxation while remaining fully aware and in control. As your conscious mind relaxes, your subconscious mind becomes receptive to helpful suggestions. You'll remember everything and remain mentally alert throughout.

How It Works

During hypnotherapy, we access the root causes of your challenges rather than just treating symptoms. By rewiring your subconscious patterns, lasting change becomes natural and effortless.
